DC Residents

  • All DC residents 21 and over are eligible for a FREE Medical Marijuana card

  •  DC resident medical marijuana cards are valid for 2 years

  • No Doctor’s recommendation needed

  • DC Driver’s License or DC Photo ID required

  • Cards are virtual; an accessible email address is needed

  • After applying, you will receive (2) emails, a confirmation of receipt followed by a digital medical card.

Get Your Card Here

Out of State/international visitors

  • Anyone 21 and over are eligible to apply for a non-resident medical marijuana card

  • No Doctor’s recommendation needed

  • Valid Driver’s License, Photo ID, or Passport required

  • Cards are virtual an accessible email address is needed

  • After Applying you will receive a payment link from DC Government

  • After payment, you will receive (2) emails, a confirmation of receipt of payment followed by a digital medical card.
