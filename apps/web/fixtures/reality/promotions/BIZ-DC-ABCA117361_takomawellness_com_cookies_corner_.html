Cookies Dispensary FAQs

What is a Cookies Corner dispensary?

Cookies Corner is a dedicated space within our dispensary that offers DC dispensary shoppers exclusive access to authentic Cookies products and merch.

Who is the owner of Cookies?

Cookies is owned by Gilbert Milam Jr, who is more widely known by his rapper/artist name Berner. Berner is considered a visionary entrepreneur and was the first executive in cannabis to be featured on a Forbes magazine cover. Berner co-founded Cookies with fellow San Franciscan Jai, one of the most recognized cannabis cultivators on the West Coast.

What Cookies products can I buy at your DC dispensary?

Takoma Wellness offers the best from the Cookies collection. You’ll find a variety of the brand’s most sought-after strains, premium concentrates, disposable vapes and carts, pre-rolls, apparel and more.

Can I order Cookies weed online?

Takoma Wellness customers can access all in-store Cookies Cannabis products via our online menu. You can browse the collection online, pick your products, and then place an order for convenient pick-up when you arrive at the dispensary. Our dispensary is also one of the few in the area that offers home cannabis delivery in Washington D.C. Simply choose delivery during checkout and enter your details.
