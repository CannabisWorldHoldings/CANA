Dispensaries in D.C.— Frequently Asked Questions

Who can shop at Takoma Wellness Center?

Adults 21+ can shop at our Washington, D.C. dispensary after completing a quick self-certification—no doctor’s visit required. We also accept valid medical cannabis cards from many states. Learn more on our Patient Resource Page.

How do I self-certify as a medical cannabis patient in D.C.?

Washington, D.C. allows adults 21+ to self-certify as medical marijuana patients. You can apply online in minutes or visit us in person for help. You’ll receive a digital card and can start shopping right away.

What cannabis products do you carry?

Our D.C. dispensary offers 500+ products, including flower, pre-rolls, vapes, concentrates, tinctures, topicals, and edibles. You’ll also find exclusive strains from Cookies—available only at Takoma Wellness.

Do you offer weed delivery in D.C.?

Yes—we offer same-day weed delivery throughout Washington, D.C. Just shop our online menu, select “Delivery” at checkout, and we’ll bring your order right to your door.

How is Takoma different from gifting shops?

Takoma Wellness Center is a fully licensed medical dispensary—operating legally in D.C. since 2013. While many gifting shops are now being required to join the regulated system, we’ve always followed strict safety and testing standards to protect our patients. When you shop with us, you can trust you’re getting legal, lab-tested cannabis backed by over a decade of experience.

Are your cannabis products made in Washington, D.C.?

Yes! As a proudly family-owned business since 2013, Takoma Wellness supports our community of local growers and producers. When you purchase cannabis from us, you strengthen the small businesses that make the District of Columbia great.
