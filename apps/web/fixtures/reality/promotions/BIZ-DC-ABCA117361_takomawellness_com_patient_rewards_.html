Patient Rewards Program

Takoma Wellness Center offers a Patient Rewards program to all!

Takoma Wellness Center offers a Rewards program that allows you to obtain discounts for your purchases. Points begin to accumulate after your first visit. Each dollar you spend equals one point. As points are earned, they may be redeemed.

150 points = $10 discount

500 points = $50 discount

1000 points = $125 discount

You can use up to 1000 points per day. Please check your Rewards Wallet to keep up-to-date with the number of points you have.
