THE OATHrules this site cannot break

Truth, by construction.

Four kinds of knowledge, never mixed.

What we observed (with source and date), what we estimate (labeled, with method), what we forecast (graded against reality later), and what we simulate (never shown as fact). A prediction can never become an inventory count here — the system physically refuses it.

Every fact has a birthday.

Facts carry the date they were observed. Stale facts get demoted honestly instead of quietly pretending to be current.

Unknown is an answer.

Empty doesn't get decorated. If hours or menus aren't verified, you'll read “unknown” — and businesses can fix it in minutes by claiming their listing.

Paid never touches organic.

Sponsored placement, when present, is labeled and lives in its own lane. Relevance is not for sale.

Corrections are welcome.

Spot something wrong? Every listing carries a report affordance; corrections are logged, attributed and traceable.
